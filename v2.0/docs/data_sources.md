# Global coastal reconstruction data stack for ReCAD v2.0

*Scope: monthly, global coastal sea-surface fCO2/pCO2 reconstruction at 1/8° (0.125°) grid, 1993–2021+ (extendable). Version numbers and URLs below were verified by web search at the time of writing (mid-2026). Items that could not be verified are flagged explicitly as **[unverified]**.*

ReCAD v2.0 is a 4× downscale of v1.1 (0.25° North-American-Atlantic coastal only) to a **global** 1/8° stack. The strategy is unchanged in spirit — a target fCO2 field driven by satellite/reanalysis covariates — but every covariate must now be global, coast-aware, and time-consistent with the SOCAT fCO2 climatology era.

## 2. Target observations: SOCAT

SOCAT is released annually. As of writing: the **SOCATv2025 database** (NCEI Accession [0304549](https://catalog.data.gov/harvest_record/2d61e53a-e344-4179-9fdd-b985873f5165/raw), released June 2025; announcement [here](https://news-oceanacidification-icc.org/2025/06/25/surface-ocean-co2-atlas-database-version-2025-socatv2025-ncei-accession-0304549/)) is the most recent formally documented release (paper preprint: Bakker et al., [ESSD 2025](https://essd.copernicus.org/preprints/essd-2025-360/)). The companion **gridded v2026 quarter-degree coastal monthly product is already served on the PMEL ERDDAP** ([SOCAT_v2026_qrtrdeg_gridded_coast_monthly](https://data.pmel.noaa.gov/socat/erddap/griddap/SOCAT_v2026_qrtrdeg_gridded_coast_monthly.html)) — the full v2026 documentation/paper is not yet published **[unverified]**.

**Use for v2.0:** the *quarter-degree coastal monthly* gridded product (introduced in v2019/v7) — variables include `coast_fco2`, `coast_fco2_std_weighted` (observation-weighted uncertainty), `coast_n_obs`, `coast_dc_*` (flags); served via ERDDAP with `.nc` subsetting, which fits a staged download. Target variable: **fCO2** (µatm, in situ). The *non-coastal* SOCAT gridded products (1° monthly, 5°×monthly climatology) should **not** be the target — they mask out most shelf cells — but are valuable for training-domain checks.

**Alternatives (benchmarks, not targets):** the **LDEO v2024** family — (i) the updated Takahashi/Sweeney ΔfCO2 climatology ([Takahashi et al. 2024, ESSD 16-2123-2024](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/16/2123/2024/) — "Updated climatological mean ΔfCO2 and net sea–air CO2 flux over the global open ocean regions", coarse 4°×5°); (ii) the LDEO pCO2-Residual Method v2024 on [Zenodo (record 13941548)](https://zenodo.org/records/13941548); (iii) the LDEO Hybrid pCO2 Product ([Zenodo 13891722](https://zenodo.org/records/13891722)). All are open-ocean interpolation products at 0.25–5° — useful as independent posterior checks and priors, unsuitable as training targets or as the basis for a 1/8° coastal product.

## 3. SSS at 1/8°

**Primary: GLORYS12v1** — CMEMS [GLOBAL_MULTIYEAR_PHY_001_030](https://data.marine.copernicus.eu/product/GLOBAL_MULTIYEAR_PHY_001_030/description), eddy-resolving 1/12° global reanalysis, daily means 1993→present (the "GLORYS12V1" description is confirmed, e.g., in the EGU [2021 abstract](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2021EGUGA..2314961L/abstract)). It provides `so` at the surface on the native tri-polar grid; aggregate daily→monthly then regrid to 1/8° with **conservative** regridding (cell-mean field). SSS from a reanalysis is the only option that (a) spans 1993→present, (b) reaches into coastal/shelf cells, and (c) is physically consistent with SST/wind. Caveat: coastal and river-plume biases exist; validate monthly fields against SOCAT cruise SSS where available.

**Fallback: satellite SSS.** RSS **SMAP Salinity V5.0** (monthly/8-day on 0.25° grid, April 2015→present; [NASA Salinity page](https://salinity.oceansciences.org/data-smap-v5.htm)) is the best-validated product, but **land–sea contamination degrades retrievals within ~100–200 km of coast** (a well-documented coastal limitation, e.g., [SMOS/coastal studies](https://www.infona.pl/resource/bwmeta1.element.ieee-art-000007730999)); SMOS (CATDS/BEC L3/L4) is worse near land. Use satellite SSS only as an independent validation/correction signal for 2015+, never inside the <=200 km coastal mask. CMEMS multi-observation SSS NRT product id changed from the v1.1-era `MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_S_SURFACE_MYNRT_015_003` to the current `MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_S_SURFACE_MYNRT_015_013` ([product page](https://data.marine.copernicus.eu/product/MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_S_SURFACE_MYNRT_015_013/description)) — **verify the live product id at build time**. v1.1 baseline **SODA 3.15.2** (0.5°, [ERDDAP SODA 3.3.1 example](https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/es/griddap/erdSoda331icemday_LonPM180.iso19115)) is superseded at 1/8°.

### 3b. SSS 备选与误差策略（v2.0 决策记录）

**问题**：卫星 L-band 反演盐度沿岸精度差；模式再分析（GLORYS）沿岸/河口也有偏差——两者都不完美，选错会把系统性误差带进 pCO₂ 重建。

**备选评估**（2026 年核实）：

| 方案 | 年代 | 分辨率 | 沿岸适用性 | 建议角色 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GLORYS12v1 `so` | 1993→ | 1/12° | 平滑、无陆地污染尖刺；但独立评估显示沿岸误差可观：自主水面艇对比 r≈0.59、RMSD≈0.96 g/kg（[IEEE 2025](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11558341)） | **主预测因子（保留）** |
| ORAS5 (ECMWF) | 1958→ | 0.25° | 不同模式家族；集合成员自带不确定度 | 敏感性/稳健性实验 |
| SMOS BEC L3 | 2010-01→ | ~25-50 km 有效 | 近岸 ~100 km 内退化 | 离岸验证/次级因子(2010+) |
| SMAP RSS V5.0 | 2015-04→ | 40/70 km | 墨西哥湾对比显示与现场"距岸无关地"相当（[Remote Sensing 10(10):1590](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/10/10/1590)）；缅因湾沿岸研究亦正面（[coastal SMAP](https://searchworks-lb.stanford.edu/articles/edsdoj__edsdoj.700bdd89aa3a4536b5bfa3f20fc4b582)） | 离岸验证/消融实验特征 |
| ESA CCI+ SSS v5.x | 2010–2023+ | 25 km 周产品 | 多任务合并优于单星，仍≥25–50 km 有效分辨率 | 开阔海域验证 |
| EN4.2.2 / ISAS / BOA | 各异 | 0.5–1° | 现场剖面客观分析；Argo 前(2005)稀疏、陆架覆盖差 | **独立校验集** |
| WOA23 月气候态 | 气候态 | 1° | 无年际变率 | 校验基线/松弛目标 |
| 多变量观测重建 1993–2016（[Frontiers 2018](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2018.00084/full)） | 1993–2016 | ~0.25-0.5° | 覆盖本重建年代 | 预测因子候选/交叉校验 |

**v2.0 决策**：
1. **主预测因子维持 GLORYS12v1 `so` 不换源**。理由：(a) 唯一覆盖 1993→今且到达沿岸格点的来源；(b) 误差场平滑（无卫星式陆地污染尖峰），对 ML 训练友好——卫星盐度误差在近岸是结构化/相关的，会恰好污染我们最关心的区域；(c) 与 SST/流场动力一致。
2. **用误差传播代替换源**：MC 输入误差保持 u_sss=0.6 PSU（v1.1 值，已宽于 GLORYS 开阔域 ~0.1–0.2）；计划增加按离岸距离缩放 u_sss 的选项（如 <50 km 内 ×1.5，对应上述沿岸 RMSD~1 PSU 的评估），并做河口区强扰动敏感性实验。
3. **独立验证（不作为预测因子）**：用 SOCAT 航迹自带的现场 `sal`（已在 socat_tracks 里）、EN4 月分析、WOA23 气候态逐区逐年核对 GLORYS SSS，统计入论文附表。
4. **稳健性实验**：用 ORAS5 盐度替换重训一轮——若重建结果对盐度源替换不敏感，即为论文的强稳健性论据。
5. **消融实验（可选）**：2015+ 时段加入离岸掩膜后的卫星 SSS 距平作为附加特征，仅当验证 RMSE 改善才保留；另做一次完全去掉 SSS 的训练以量化其贡献。

**实现状态**：u_sss 已在 `UncertaintyConfig.input_uncertainties` 中可配；距岸缩放为待办小项。

## 4. SST at 1/8°

**Keep OISST v2.1** ([NCEI C01606](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.ncdc:C01606); daily, 0.25°, 1981-09→present) as primary for the full 1993–2021 record and direct v1.1 continuity. No OISST v3 release was found **[unverified]**.

**Alternatives to evaluate:** (a) **MUR SST v4.x** — 0.01° daily 2002→present ([CoastWatch ERDDAP jplMURSST41](https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/zh-CN/griddap/jplMURSST41_Lon0360.iso19115)); new v4.2 series incl. 0.25° MUR25 ([NCEI GHRSST-MUR25-JPL-L4-GLOB](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.nodc:GHRSST-MUR25-JPL-L4-GLOB)); (b) **ESA SST CCI/C3S reprocessed L4** at 0.05° ([CMEMS 010_024](https://data.marine.copernicus.eu/product/SST_GLO_SST_L4_REP_OBSERVATIONS_010_024/description); SST_cci v3.0, [CEDA](https://catalogue.ceda.ac.uk/uuid/62800d3d2227449085b430b503d36b01/)). MUR/CCI give real coastal detail (MUR's 1 km resolves SST fronts OISST smears); trade-offs: MUR starts 2002 and has higher noise; CCI/C3S is reprocessed-consistent but with a delayed update cadence. **Recommendation:** OISST v2.1 primary (record length + v1.1 parity), MUR 1 km as a coastal refinement/uncertainty input for 2002+; CCI/C3S as an internal consistency check. Pipeline: daily fields → **area-conservative regrid to 0.125°** (xesmf `conservative`) → monthly mean and monthly standard deviation (keep both for uncertainty propagation).

## 5. SSH

**CMEMS SEALEVEL_GLO_PHY_L4_MY_008_047 remains the current global gridded delayed-time sea-level product** (0.25°, daily, 1993→present, all-satellite merged SSALTO/Duacs; variables `adt`, `sla`, geostrophic velocities; confirmed current by [CMEMS/FEDEO](https://fedeo.ceos.org/collections/series/items/SEALEVEL_GLO_PHY_L4_MY_008_047) and [recent literature use, ESSD 2025](https://essd.copernicus.org/preprints/essd-2025-384/)). Access via CMEMS (free registration) or AVISO+. A global **1/8°** gridded L4 altimetry product could not be verified **[unverified]** — regional finer grids exist (e.g., Mediterranean/Arctic), not global. Recommendation: keep 0.25° delayed-time `adt` (not `sla`, to retain the mean dynamic topography signal) as in v1.1, monthly-mean and regrid to 1/8° with **bilinear** interpolation; treat coastal/shelf SLA values as lower-confidence (cross-track Land-masked cells; check `err` fields). The climate-mode variant [SEALEVEL_GLO_PHY_CLIMATE_L4_MY_008_057](https://fedeo.ceos.org/series/concepts/sciencekeyword/1ed24fe1-d0d5-46d1-8d22-8ac25d289c75/SEALEVEL_GLO_PHY_CLIMATE_L4_MY_008_057) can serve as an extended (1993→present, more satellites) alternative.

## 6. Wind

**CCMP v3.1** (6-hourly, 0.25°, RSS/NASA, [v3.1 record](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/rss-ccmp-6-hourly-10-meter-surface-winds-level-4-version-3-1); [UCAR guide](https://climatedataguide.ucar.edu/climate-data/ccmp-cross-calibrated-multi-platform-wind-vector-analysis)) is the v2.0 recommendation: cross-calibrated multi-platform (scatterometer + ECMWF background), 6-hourly sampling that integrates cleanly into monthly mean flux calculations, and direct continuity with the v1.1 baseline (CCMP v2.0/v3.1). A **v3.2** release could not be confirmed **[unverified]** — check the [RSS CCMP page](https://www.remss.com/measurements/ccmp/) at build time. **ERA5** (hourly, 0.25°, 10-m wind; Hersbach et al. 2020, QJRMS 146:1999) is the natural fallback and is stronger in coastal cells (coastal-land parameterization and consistent diurnal cycle) — recommend pulling ERA5 alongside and doing a coastal skill intercomparison. Store **vector components** (u10, v10) and also monthly-mean of **scalar speed** (needed for gas-transfer velocity; vector-mean speed would alias the turbulence signal).

## 7. Atmospheric CO2

Use the **NOAA GML Marine Boundary Layer (MBL) reference product** ([gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/mbl/mbl.html](https://www.gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/mbl/mbl.html); data server [here](https://gmd-int.cmdl.noaa.gov/ccgg/data/getdata.php?gas=co2)) — monthly, 12-month smoothed, zonal-mean-by-latitude xCO2 (dry-air mole fraction). This is the same source as v1.1 (NOAA GML/ESRL) and is expected to remain optimal: it is the only global product tied to the calibrated surface network. **Conversion to pCO2air at sea surface temperature** (the "pCO2 of air in equilibrium with seawater at in-situ SST/SSS", needed for ΔfCO2): pCO2air = xCO2 × (P_atm − pH2O(SST,SSS)) × fugacity/activity correction, computed with **PyCO2SYS** (Humphreys et al. 2022, MethodsX) using *in-vivo seawater* constants (as v1.1 did). Caveats: MBL is zonal-mean (no longitude structure — acceptable at monthly/1/8° scale for air-sea gradient purposes); use a single global mean sea-level pressure field (e.g., ERA5 monthly MSLP) or constant 1013 hPa with sensitivity testing; the MBL file set is recalculated yearly, so pin the version in the staging manifest.

## 8. Coastal mask

**Recommendation: GSHHG v2.3.7 + distance-to-coastline ≤ 200 km (configurable), computed on the target 0.125° grid.** The mask is built once by: (1) rasterizing the GSHHG `L1` (land) polygons at 0.125°; (2) computing the distance from every ocean cell's center to the nearest coastline (e.g., `gdal_proximity` or a KD-tree over the GSHHG vertex set); (3) retaining ocean cells with distance ≤ threshold and depth > 0.

**Reproducibility argument:** GSHHG is a fixed, versioned, global vector coastline set ([2.3.7 on Zenodo](https://zenodo-rdm.web.cern.ch/records/7007502); Wessel & Smith, JGR 1996) independent of any observational product, so the same script + pinned GSHHG version + pinned grid yields a byte-identical mask on any machine — ship the mask itself inside the zarr store with its provenance (GSHHG version, distance function, threshold) as metadata.

**Cross-checks (not primary):** the SOCAT quarter-degree coastal product's own cell selection (it defines its coastal domain internally) and NASA's 66 **[Large Marine Ecosystems polygons](https://maps.edc.uri.edu/ArcGIS/rest/services/LME/LMEWebMap/MapServer/2)** (ecologically meaningful but coarser and less reproducible). Run sensitivity experiments at 50/100/200/400 km; ~200 km matches typical SOCAT coastal-grid extent and v1.1 practice.

## 9. Data volume, compute, and staging plan

**Grid and volume.** 0.125° global: 2880 lon × 1440 lat = 4.15 M cells; monthly float32 = **16.6 MB/variable**. 1993–2021 (348 months): **5.8 GB per monthly variable**; the core stack (fco2 target+std, sst+sd, sss, adt, u10/v10+speed, pco2air, mask) ≈ **45–55 GB raw float32**, ~2–4× less as compressed zarr. Daily inputs are the volume drivers — do *not* store daily fields at 1/8°: e.g., OISST daily at 0.25° ≈ 4.1 MB/day (~45 GB/30 yr) and CCMP 6-hourly ≈ 16.6 MB/day (~180 GB/30 yr at 0.25°); consume them lazily, regrid, and persist only monthly aggregates.

**Store schema (single standardized zarr).** One consolidated zarr store `recad_v2_monthly.zarr` with shared `time` (cftime monthly), `lat` (−89.9375…89.9375), `lon` (0.125° steps), variables per the overview table; CF/COARDS attrs per variable incl. `source`, `source_version`, `grid_mapping`, `_FillValue`, `units`; dataset-level `history`, `pipeline_scripts_sha256`, `mask_provenance`. Chunking: `{'time': 36, 'lat': 180, 'lon': 360}` (~9.3 MB/chunk) — good balance for dask/xarray lazy reads on a laptop-to-cluster range; use `zarr` v2/v3 with default gzip or `blosc-zstd` and `consolidated=True`.

**Staging plan (download once → standardized store).**
1. *Pin & license:* register free CMEMS and NASA Earthdata accounts; note licensing (CMEMS Copernicus licence; RSS data via Earthdata; SOCAT CC BY 4.0 on NCEI — verify at download; NOAA GML public domain). Keep a `MANIFEST.json` (product, version, DOI/URL, download date, checksum).
2. *Raw fetch:* ERDDAP `.nc` subset (SOCAT coastal; OISST) and `motu-client`/OPeNDAP (CMEMS GLORYS, sea level); CCMP via Earthdata; MBL via GML file server; GSHHG/GEBCO static files.
3. *Regrid to 0.125°:* conservative for fCO2/SST/SSS (cell-mean fields), bilinear for adt and wind components; xesmf with precomputed weights.
4. *Temporal aggregation:* daily→monthly mean (+std, +n_obs where available); wind speed mean computed from *hourly/6-hourly scalar speed* before averaging.
5. *Write zarr* with the schema above; verify with `xr.open_zarr(..., consolidated=True)` round-trip; record per-variable footprint and generation time.

Compute is comfortably laptop-parallelizable at monthly granularity (348×~10 variables ≈ 3.5k chunks); for faster iteration, consider a coastal-subset preview store first, then the full global store.
